UC and CSU are test-blind. The Ivies, Claremont McKenna, and the test-required private universities (Stanford, Caltech, MIT, Johns Hopkins, Georgetown, Carnegie Mellon, Notre Dame) require the SAT or ACT for fall 2028 entry, with admitted middle ranges of roughly 1450–1580. The other private schools are test-optional: submit a score only if it is at or above their middle range; otherwise GPA, rigor, essays, and recommendations carry the file. UC and CSU recalculate GPA from 10th- and 11th-grade A–G courses with up to 8 semesters of honors/AP bonus points (capped), so use the weighted GPA from those years (a 10th-grade academic weighted GPA is a fair stand-in until junior-year grades post). Main campus images are stored locally after START_TOUR.bat runs. Ivy League and liberal arts entries use the same federal data sources as the California campuses. Academic ranking uses broad tiers because UCs and CSUs are often ranked in different categories; each campus also shows its specific 2026 ranking context. UC national ranks use U.S. News 2026. CSU notes use current U.S. News, TIME, Money, or Wall Street Journal/College Pulse results where applicable. Rankings are one screening factor, not a substitute for program fit. Cost figures assume a California resident: the published annual in-state tuition and fees and the school’s estimated housing and food budget from the U.S. Department of Education College Scorecard (IPEDS); actual cost depends on housing choice, meal plan, and financial aid. Re-run fetch_costs.py to refresh them. “Tours & open houses” opens each campus’s own visit page, where tour dates, preview days, and open-house registration are posted; those pages were checked in September 2026. Application facts (fees, filing windows, essay and test policies) are from the UC Admissions and Cal State Apply sites as of September 2026 (fall 2027 cycle; fees and dates for fall 2028 are normally announced the following summer); confirm on the application site before filing. The ranking filter is cumulative: choosing “Top 50 or better” also shows Top 20 campuses. Value figures (earnings, debt, graduation rate) come from the College Scorecard’s most recent release; the payback measure is this page’s own arithmetic on those figures, not an official statistic. Admission likelihood combines each campus’s overall admit rate (College Scorecard, most recent year) with where the entered GPA falls against the campus’s published middle-50% GPA range of admitted first-year students (UC Admissions, fall 2026 admitted class; Cal Poly SLO and SDSU from their own admitted-student profiles; other impacted CSUs estimated). Non-impacted CSU campuses admit nearly every eligible California applicant. Ivy League odds use the admit rate and the admitted middle-50% SAT range (College Scorecard); all eight require the SAT or ACT for fall 2028 entry. Liberal arts colleges and private universities use admit rate and GPA, with the SAT counted only where it helps (test-optional) or is required. Private-university rankings are U.S. News 2026 National Universities (University of Portland is ranked as a Regional University, West). Liberal arts rankings are U.S. News 2026 National Liberal Arts Colleges; Reed does not participate in that ranking. Merit-aid figures (share of freshmen without financial need who received a merit scholarship, and the average award) come from each school’s 2024-25 Common Data Set, section H2A, as compiled by College Transitions (November 2025); the merit chance scales that share by where your GPA and SAT fall against the admitted range and is a rough guide only. The majors filter uses the federal IPEDS completions survey for 2023-24: a field counts as offered when the campus awarded bachelor’s degrees in that family that year, and the percentage is the field’s share of all bachelor’s degrees the campus awarded, a rough guide to program size. Check the campus catalog for specific majors within a field. Climate categories come from ten years (2015–2024) of daily weather at each campus from the Open-Meteo ERA5 archive: winter and summer mean highs, annual rain and snow, and sunshine hours, sorted by the rules listed in build_climate.py. Religious affiliation is what each institution reports to IPEDS (Institutional Characteristics survey); the note in parentheses on how active the affiliation is in campus life is this tour’s own. Student-to-faculty ratios and first-year retention are from the federal IPEDS fall enrollment survey (fall 2023); town populations are 2020 Census counts; the nature rating and beach distances are this tour’s own, with the rubric documented in build_profile.py. The fit score weighs only the survey items you mark important and is a preference tool, not an admissions measure. It is a rough screening band, not a prediction: UC reviews 13 factors, and selectivity varies sharply by major.
