Pathfinder Survey

Answer honestly about what you enjoy, what you're good at, and what matters to you. At the end you'll get a three-letter interest code, a map of how your interests fit together, and a list of college majors and careers worth a closer look.

    You're looking at example answers from a made-up student. Press Clear form to clear them and take the survey yourself.

    Part 1 of 5

    Activities you'd enjoy

    How would you feel about doing each of these? Don't worry about whether you're good at it yet. Only think about whether it sounds fun.

    Part 2 of 5

    Your strengths

    Rate yourself compared to other people your age. Think about what teachers, coaches, and friends say you're good at.

    Part 3 of 5

    Classes you like

    Pick every class or activity you look forward to, even if your grade in it isn't perfect.

    Part 4 of 5

    What matters to you in a future job

    Pick your top three
    After high school, which paths are you open to?

    Pick as many as you like. Careers that fit these paths get highlighted in your results.

    How do you like to work?

    Pick a spot between the two answers. The middle dot means no preference. These don't change your interest code; they flag careers that fit how you like to work, and ones with a catch.

    Part 5 of 5

    A few questions to think about

    These don't change your score. If you name a job, it shows up in your results under “Careers from your own answers.” They're also good starting points for a conversation with a parent, counselor, or mentor, and they get included when you copy your results.
