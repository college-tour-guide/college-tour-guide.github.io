College Virtual Tour
====================
Open index.html in any browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox). Keep the photos folder
and the campus_*.js files next to it.

Type your own weighted GPA (10th-11th grade, UC/CSU style) and SAT if you have one;
everything you enter, your Visit/Maybe/Skip choices, and the fit survey stay in your own
browser and are never uploaded anywhere.

The map needs an internet connection; everything else works offline.

Data sources: U.S. Department of Education College Scorecard and IPEDS, UC Admissions,
each school's Common Data Set (merit aid), U.S. News 2026 rankings, and the schools'
own admissions pages, as of September 2026. See the notes at the bottom of the page.
